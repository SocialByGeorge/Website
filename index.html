About George

THE MAN BEHIND THE STRATEGY.

I’m George, 26, based in Maldon, Essex. Social By George started because my sister is a hairdresser working in high-end salons, and like a lot of brilliant beauty professionals, she was too busy with clients to give Instagram the time it needed.

I started running her hair account, and that gave me a much clearer look at the gap between doing great work and actually presenting it well online.

That became the idea behind Social By George: helping beauty, grooming and wellness businesses look as professional online as they are in real life.
Same passion. Different perspective.

FAQs

BEFORE WE WORK TOGETHER.

Do I have to film content myself?

For most monthly services, you’ll still need to capture some raw content from your business because you’re there with the clients, services and results. I can tell you exactly what to film and how to send it over. Local content-day support can also be quoted separately.

Do you only work with businesses in Essex?

No. I’m based in Maldon, Essex and can support local businesses in person where relevant, but Social By George also works fully online.

Do you guarantee followers or bookings?

No. Social media doesn’t work like that. I focus on stronger strategy, better content, clearer positioning, consistency and ongoing optimisation. Those are the things we can control and improve.

Do you run paid ads?

Not currently. The focus is organic social media strategy, content and management.

What platforms do you manage?

Instagram is the main focus at launch. Other platforms can be discussed depending on the package and what your business actually needs.

How do I get started?

DM @socialbygeorge on Instagram with a little about your business, what you’re struggling with and what you want social media to achieve.
