FAQs

BEFORE WE WORK TOGETHER.

Do I have to film content myself?

For most monthly services you will need to supply some real photos or video from the business because you are there with the clients, services and results. I can give you clear shot lists and filming direction. Essex content support can also be quoted separately.

Do you only work with businesses in Essex?

No. I’m based in Maldon, Essex, but Social By George can work fully online with businesses elsewhere in the UK.

Do you guarantee followers, enquiries or bookings?

No. No responsible social media manager can guarantee specific organic results. I focus on the things we can control: strategy, content quality, positioning, consistency, calls to action and ongoing optimisation.

How long should I give social media management?

For full management, I usually recommend allowing around three months to establish the strategy, build consistency and collect enough performance data to make useful decisions. Exact terms are confirmed in your proposal.

How does content approval work?

Content is planned and shared through the agreed approval process before publishing. This keeps you in control of service details, pricing, offers and how the brand is represented.

Do you need my password?

Where possible, I prefer official platform permissions and business access tools rather than casual password sharing. The safest practical setup for your account is agreed during onboarding.

Do you manage comments and DMs?

Community support can be included where agreed, but detailed client enquiries, medical questions, pricing decisions or booking conversations may still need to be handled by you or your team.

Do you run paid ads?

Not currently. The launch offer focuses on organic social media strategy, content and management.

What platforms do you manage?

Instagram is the main launch focus. Other platforms can be discussed depending on the package and what your business actually needs.

When will you reply to me?

Client communication is handled during normal working days. Exact communication channels, approval deadlines and expected response times are set during onboarding so there are clear boundaries on both sides.

How do I get started?

DM @socialbygeorge on Instagram. Tell me what you do, where you are based, what is frustrating you about social media and which package you are interested in.
