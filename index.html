FAQs

GOOD QUESTION.

Who do you work with?

Social By George is built for hair, nails, lashes, aesthetics, grooming and other beauty-led businesses. The goal is always the same: clearer positioning, stronger content and a social presence that feels as good as the service.

Do I need to create the content myself?

That depends on the service. For management clients, I can give you clear capture instructions so you film the raw material while I handle the strategy, structure, copy, design and publishing plan.

Can you guarantee followers or bookings?

No one can honestly guarantee a specific number. I focus on building a stronger system: better content, more qualified reach, clearer calls to action, consistent reporting and ongoing optimisation.

How do I enquire?

DM @socialbygeorge on Instagram. Tell me what your business does, what you currently struggle with and what you'd like social media to achieve.
