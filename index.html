FAQ

Before you DM me.

Do I need professional photos already?

No. We can work with good-quality phone content, existing photos and planned shot lists. In-person content shoots can be quoted separately.

Do you film content at my business?

Content shoots are separate from the monthly management price. That keeps the entry packages affordable and lets you add filming only when you need it.

Why are your starting rates lower?

They are founding-client rates while Social By George builds its first client case studies. They will not stay at this level permanently.

Can you guarantee bookings or follower growth?

No responsible social media manager can guarantee a specific number of bookings or followers. The focus is strong strategy, consistent content and improving performance over time.
